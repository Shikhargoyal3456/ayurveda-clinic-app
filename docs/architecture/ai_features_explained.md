# AI Features Explained

## Tongue Diagnosis
This feature lets a clinician upload or capture a tongue image and receive an Ayurvedic interpretation in seconds. In the U.S. startup market, it maps to a multimodal clinical imaging assistant like Viz.ai or Hippocratic AI, and it can become a premium add-on for specialist clinics.

## Voice to Action
Instead of transcription alone, the assistant turns speech into a draft case sheet, medicine suggestions, a prescription, and a follow-up date in one workflow. This is similar to ambient scribes like Suki or Abridge, and it creates monetizable time savings for doctors while increasing visit throughput.

## Churn Prediction
The app flags patients who have not visited recently and surfaces them in a dashboard with one-click reminder actions. That resembles operational AI from Qventus or Corti, and the revenue angle is higher retention, more repeat visits, and better clinic utilization.

## Billing Codes
When a prescription is finalized, the system generates ICD-11 and AYUSH-style coding guidance and stores it with the record. This is comparable to automation from CodaMetrix or Fathom, and it supports insurance workflows, billing accuracy, and future claims-based monetization.

## Rx Recommendations
For a new patient, the app searches prior cases and outcomes to show the top similar histories and the medicines that helped them. This follows the personalization model used by companies like K Health or Tempus, and it can drive inventory-aware prescribing, bulk purchasing insights, and clinic-owner upsells.
